This release only includes 64-bit plugins.

For VST 2:
	Copy "Signalizer.vst" into your VSTPlugins folder.

For VST 3:
	Copy "Signalizer.vst3" into your VST3 folder.

For standalone:
	Copy "Signalizer" to any location you prefer.

-------------------

If you're unsure about where your VSTPlugins folder is, it can be very custom and I'd advise to research the internet for your particular DAW if you didn't choose it yourself.

Typically VST3 folder will be at: C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

If you're having issues, you can try above again but download the debug PDBs and place the associated .pdb file next to Signalizer.dll inside the Signalizer.vst[3] folder.
This will produce more precise error messages and log output.
